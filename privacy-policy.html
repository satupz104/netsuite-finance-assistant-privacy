Privacy Policy - NetSuite Finance Assistant

Last Updated: April 25, 2026

Overview

NetSuite Finance Assistant ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our Chrome extension.

Information We Collect

WE DO NOT COLLECT ANY PERSONAL INFORMATION OR USER DATA. All extension functionality operates locally within your browser.

Local Storage Usage

The extension uses Chrome's local storage API to save your personal preferences:

  • Theme settings (light/dark mode, background colors)
  • Sidebar collapse/expand state
  • Section visibility preferences

This data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted or accessed by us.

How We Use Information

Local Functionality Only

  • User preferences are stored locally to maintain your chosen settings across browser sessions
  • No data is sent to external servers or third parties
  • All NetSuite data processing happens locally in your browser

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT:

  • Sell or transfer user data to third parties
  • Share data with advertisers or analytics services
  • Use data for purposes unrelated to the extension's functionality
  • Transmit any NetSuite transaction data or personal information

Data Security

  • All data remains on your local device
  • No external servers or databases are used
  • Extension follows Chrome's security best practices

Third-Party Services

The extension does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected in the extension's updated version.

Contact Information

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store support channels.

Compliance

This extension complies with:

  • Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies
  • Google Privacy Policy requirements
  • All applicable data protection regulations

By using NetSuite Finance Assistant, you agree to this privacy policy.
